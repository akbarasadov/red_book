Endangered Animals

Animals that are disappearing from our planet

Many animals are becoming endangered because of human activities and changes in nature.

What Does “Endangered” Mean?

An endangered animal is an animal whose population is becoming very small and may disappear in the future.

  • 🌳 Loss of natural habitats
  • 🏹 Hunting and poaching
  • 🌊 Pollution
  • 🌡️ Climate change
  • 🍃 Lack of food

The Red Book

The Red Book is a special list of rare and endangered animals and plants.

  • 📕 It contains information about endangered species.
  • 🐾 It helps people protect rare animals.
  • 🌱 It includes endangered plants too.
  • 🛡️ It encourages nature conservation.

The Red Book reminds us that every species is important.

Tigers

Tigers are one of the most beautiful and powerful animals in the world.

  • They live mainly in Asia.
  • Their natural habitats are disappearing.
  • Illegal hunting is a serious threat.
  • Protecting forests can help save tigers.

🐢

Sea Turtles

Sea turtles live in oceans around the world.

  • Plastic pollution is dangerous for them.
  • They can eat plastic by mistake.
  • Their eggs are threatened on some beaches.
  • Clean oceans and beaches can protect them.

🦏

Rhinoceroses

Rhinoceroses are large and powerful animals.

  • Some rhino species are critically endangered.
  • Poaching is a major threat.
  • They need safe places to live.
  • Wildlife protection is very important.

How Can We Help?

  • 🌳 Protect forests and natural habitats.
  • 🚯 Reduce plastic and pollution.
  • 🐾 Support wildlife protection.
  • 🛑 Never buy products from endangered animals.
  • 📢 Teach others about endangered species.

Every animal is important. Let's protect our planet! 🌍
